The Conference Memoirs of a Veteran AI Scholar

A first-person, story-driven, mathematically formal textbook narrated by an aging AI researcher attending landmark conferences over decades. Blending personal conference floor memoirs and technical opinions with formal mathematical derivations (tailored for a Mathematics graduate) and ground-zero Python/PyTorch implementations.

"I have sat in cold conference halls from NIPS 1988 to NeurIPS 2024. I watched perceptrons stumble, saw vectors discover meaning in Lake Tahoe, stood in Long Beach as transformers were born, and watched DPO and DeepSeek collapse complex reinforcement learning into pure mathematical elegance."
